Why ship from Florida

Jacksonville: your gateway to the Southeast & East Coast

The Southeast is one of the fastest-growing regions in the country. Stocking inventory in Jacksonville, in Florida's northeast corner, means shorter, cheaper ground shipments to customers from Miami to Atlanta to the Carolinas, and easy reach up the East Coast.
