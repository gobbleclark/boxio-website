How our 3PL works

From your store to your customer's door

Connect your store, send us inventory, and we take it from there.

01

Connect your store in minutes

Link Shopify, Amazon, Walmart, WooCommerce, BigCommerce and more. Your products and orders import automatically, with no spreadsheets or manual entry.

  • Native integrations for major platforms & marketplaces
  • API integrations for custom builds
02

Send inventory to Utah, Florida, or both

Ship your stock to the warehouse closest to your customers. Every unit is received into our barcode scanning system, so counts are accurate from day one.

  • Barcode-scanned receiving reduces errors
  • We'll help you decide where to stock what
03

Orders picked, packed & shipped fast

When an order comes in, our team picks, packs and ships it with the right carrier for speed and cost, with options for 2-day express and global expedited delivery.

  • UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL and more
  • Kitting, bundling and custom packaging
04

See inventory and shipments in real time

Our in-house software gives you live inventory counts, order status and tracking, so you always know what's in stock and what's on the way.

  • Real-time inventory counts
  • Order tracking pushed back to your store
