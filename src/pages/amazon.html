Amazon FBA prep

FBA-ready inventory, sent in when you need it

Amazon is strict about how inventory arrives. We prep every unit and carton to Amazon's requirements and ship it into fulfillment centers, so your shipments get checked in instead of stuck.

  • Receiving & inspection with barcode scanning as inventory arrives
  • FNSKU labeling on every unit
  • Poly-bagging and bundling where Amazon requires it
  • Carton labels and inbound shipments to Amazon fulfillment centers
  • Ongoing replenishment from your inventory with us, so you don't run out in FBA
Merchant-fulfilled (FBM)

Ship your own Amazon orders, on time, every time

For products you fulfill yourself, FBM orders sync from Seller Central to our warehouse automatically. We ship them on time with valid tracking, which protects your seller metrics.

  • Automatic order import from Seller Central
  • Tracking uploaded to Amazon as soon as each order ships
  • Seller Fulfilled Prime sellers supported
  • Oversized or slow-moving SKUs that cost too much to store in FBA
