Why ship from Utah

The crossroads of the West

Our Utah fulfillment center sits in Utah County, just off I-15 and about an hour south of Salt Lake City. That central western location keeps ground transit short to the customers that matter most to West-focused brands.

  • Fast ground reach to California, the Pacific Northwest, Arizona, Nevada, Colorado and the Mountain West.
  • Lower shipping costs by shipping ground to customers who'd otherwise need air.
  • Local team for Utah brands. Work with a 3PL in your own backyard, from Provo to Salt Lake City.
